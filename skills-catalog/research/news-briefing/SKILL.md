# News briefing

Write in the user's language. Requires web search.

## Steps
1. Check today's date. Search for news in the requested field from the last 1-7 days (put the date or "this week" in the queries).
2. Collect 8-15 candidate items, then keep the 5-8 that matter most to the user's goals.
3. For each: a bold one-line headline in your own words, 1-2 sentences on why it matters, source name and link, publication date.
4. Group by theme if there are several.
5. End with "What to watch" — 1-3 things likely to develop next.

## Rules
- Only items you actually read; no invented news.
- Skip duplicates of the same story; link the best source.
- Save to docs/briefings/<date>.md.
