# Business email

Write in the user's language and match the relationship (formal for new contacts, friendly for colleagues).

## Structure
- **Subject**: the purpose in under 8 words, e.g. "Approval needed: Q3 budget by Fri".
- **First sentence**: the point or the request.
- **Body**: only the context the reader needs; short paragraphs or bullets.
- **The ask**: exactly what you need, from whom, by when.
- **Closing**: thanks and sign-off.

## Rules
- One email, one main topic.
- Re-read for tone: polite, direct, no blame.
- The agent cannot send email: save the draft to drafts/email-<topic>.md for the user to send.
